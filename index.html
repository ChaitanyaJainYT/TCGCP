📜 How to play

Setup

  • 2–8 players. The deck (64 cards) is dealt face-down: 12 each for 2–5 players, 10 each for 6, 8 each for 7–8.
  • Keep your pile face-down. No peeking! 👀

Playing

  • On your turn, flip your top card onto the centre pile and say the next word: Taco → Cat → Goat → Cheese → Pizza → Taco…
  • Match! If the card matches the word just said, everyone slaps the pile. The last to slap takes the whole pile (bottom of their pile) and starts again with “Taco”.
  • Flinch! Slap when there’s nothing to slap and you take the pile.
  • Said the wrong word? Press “😬 Wrong word?” and pick the player who mis-said the mantra — they take the centre pile and restart.
  • Out of cards? You still take your turn and say the word — you stay in the game.
  • Win: have zero cards and be among the players who correctly slap the pile.

Special cards 🦍🦫🦄

  • Gorilla — beat your chest with both hands.
  • Groundhog — knock both hands on the table.
  • Narwhal — hands above your head like a horn.

Everyone does the gesture, then slaps — last to confirm picks up the pile.

Controls in this app

  • Keys: each player has a key shown on their seat (A S D F J K L ;) — it flips, slaps and confirms gestures.
  • Tap your seat to flip / slap / confirm.
  • Click the centre pile to slap — credited to the nearest player (mouse/touch).
  • Everyone responds: on a match or a special card, play waits until every player has slapped / confirmed the gesture — no countdown. The last to respond takes the pile.
  • Manual mode: slaps and gestures are settled offline — press “Give centre cards…” and choose the player who takes the pile from the pop-up.
  • Said the wrong word? press “😬 Wrong word?” (available in both modes) and choose the player — they take the centre pile.

Settings ⚙️

  • Word hint — show or hide the mantra and the word to say.
  • Manual pile resolution — switch between waiting for everyone to respond and the manual “give the pile” button. Your choices are remembered.
